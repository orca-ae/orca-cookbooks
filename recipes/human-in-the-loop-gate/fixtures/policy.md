# Expense approval policy

Decide each expense with one of: approve, reject, escalate.

- Approve when the amount is at or under 200 USD and the category is one of
  travel, meals, or supplies.
- Reject when the receipt is missing, or the category is `entertainment`.
- Escalate anything over 200 USD, and anything you are unsure about.
- Escalate every expense from a contractor regardless of amount, because the
  contractor budget is tracked separately.

Never approve two expenses in a single call. Decide them one at a time.
