# Pricing rules (internal)

Base list price is 1,200 USD per seat per year.

Volume tiers, applied to the whole order:
- 1-24 seats: no discount
- 25-99 seats: 10%
- 100-499 seats: 18%
- 500+ seats: 25%

Term:
- Annual: no change
- Two-year prepaid: a further 8% off the post-volume price
- Monthly: add 15% to the post-volume price

Floors that cannot be crossed without VP approval:
- Never below 640 USD per seat per year.
- Never combine the 500+ tier with two-year prepaid below 700 USD per seat.

Support tiers are priced separately and are never discounted.
